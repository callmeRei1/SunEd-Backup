INFORMATION ABOUT DUAL DIPLOMA PROGRAM

  • Compulsory courses in the Dual Diploma Program:
    • 6 Language Arts courses (equivalent to 3 credits)
    • 2 courses on American Civics and Government (equivalent to 1 credit)
    • 2 elective courses (equivalent to 1 credit).
  • Duration: depending on each student's training pathway, average training time is from 2 to 4 years for 10 courses.
BENEFITS OF DUAL DIPLOMA PROGRAM
In addition to VN High School Diploma, with an American High School Diploma, students can
1. Apply to US and international universities
2. Have advantages in scholarship applications for international universities
3. Prepare themselves for international and domestic university environment
4. Gain opportunity to work at multinational companies or international organizations.
The Benefits of the Dual-Degree Program for Parents and Students at SunEd
• Receive an American High School Diploma upon completion of the Dual Diploma Program along with the Vietnamese High School Diploma
• Save cost compared to studying abroad or studying at bilingual schools or international schools in Vietnam
• Have international study and career opportunities: With an American High School Diploma, students can easily apply to universities or foreign or multinational companies, meeting the needs of the global labor market
• Get FREE advice on major and university selection, FREE career guidance
• Receive recommendation letters from American teachers and from Advantages School International, USA, to supplement the application for US universities (upon request)
• Get guidance and support on writing application essays for universities and scholarships; and other educational services for FREE
