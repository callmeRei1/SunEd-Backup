PROGRAM OVERVIEW

  • Class size: 15-20 students
  • Entry requirements: students aged 13 and above with a minimum English proficiency of A2+ CEFR
  • Teaching staff: 100% American teachers
  • Technology integrating traditional and modern methods
  • Learning format: online
  • Timetable: flexible
  • Diverse courses from language arts, mathematics, science to social sciences, music, etc. to improve English skills, expand general knowledge.
  • Course duration: 18+ weeks/course
    • 2 hours/week with American teachers
    • 2 hours/week of practice with support from Vietnamese facilitators
    • 2+ hours/week of self-study on the platform

ADVANTAGES OF ONLINE ENGLISH PROGRAM at SUNED
